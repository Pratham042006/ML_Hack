# Routing result reconciliation

Two recorded student-test runs use the same aggregate label counts but report different classifier-only and midpoint totals: 4,972 versus 4,936 and 4,584 versus 4,430 respectively. Both report 7,797 projected agreements. Local reruns of trips 00000007 and 00000040 reproduce the first run. The other machine's individual predictions and exact input hashes were not available for a row-by-row comparison, so the cause is not established.

Investigation on the local candidate generator found that 1,351 / 1,354 observations on trip 00000007 and 2,408 / 2,995 on trip 00000040 have the two nearest arc distances within 1e-8 metres. Reverse/near-identical arcs and numerical tie handling are therefore worth investigating, but these counts do not prove they caused the discrepancy. Do not mix the two result tables or change tie handling based on student-test outcomes; compare prediction and candidate hashes across systems first.

The five-model student-test comparison in `results/heading_student_summary.json` refits each model in the source CSV's row order. The earlier paired routing comparison used a separately refitted histogram model after sorting training rows. On a local trip these two histogram artifacts produced different choices; they must not be treated as identical. The pinned demo artifact is a metadata-preserving copy of the earlier evaluated paired-routing artifact, not the separate five-model refit. Training-order sensitivity and early-stopping behavior require investigation before interpreting small differences between models. Both artifacts use the same frozen heading training export.
